----------------------------------------------------------------------
Total pixels: 40,639,315

Test sample: 6,000 pixels
Target: Extreme heat (LST > 32.24C at 90th percentile)
Features: LST, NDVI, NDBI, Brightness, PopDensity, Bio1

FEATURES ENGINEERED
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. LST (Land Surface Temperature) - from Landsat ST_B10
2. NDVI (Vegetation Index) - (NIR-Red)/(NIR+Red)
3. NDBI (Built-up Index) - (SWIR1-NIR)/(SWIR1+NIR)
4. Brightness - (Red+NIR)/2
5. Population Density - from SEDAC GPWv4
6. Bio1 (Annual Mean Temp) - from WorldClim 2.1

MACHINE LEARNING APPROACH
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Methods Used:
  - Ensemble Learning: Multiple models trained together
  - Random Forest: 100 trees with max depth 15
  - Logistic Regression: Baseline linear classifier
  - XGBoost: Gradient boosting with 100 estimators
  - Voting Classifier: Soft voting ensemble of 3 models
  - Cross-Validation: 5-fold stratified CV for robustness
