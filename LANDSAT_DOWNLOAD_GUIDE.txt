## �� ❓ WHY YOU WERE CONFUSED
EarthExplorer lists Level-2 products separately:
- `LANDSAT/LC08/C02/T1_L2` = FULL product (SR + ST + QA + Angle) ← **WE NEED THIS**
- `LANDSAT/LC08/C02/T1_L2_SR` = SR-only product (SR + QA + Angle only) ← **NO ST BANDS**
- `LANDSAT/LC08/C02/T1_L2_ST` = ST-only product (ST + QA + Angle only) ← Rarely used

You were looking at the SR-only product group by mistake. The FULL product group (without _SR) is what contains ST_B10.

## �� 🚨 CRITICAL REMINDER
When expanding the product groups, **DO NOT** expand:
- Anything with `_SR` at the end (Surface Reflectance only)
- Anything with `_ST` at the end (Surface Temperature only - if it exists)
- Anything with `C01` (Collection 1 - older processing)
- Anything with `L1TP` (Level-1 - raw data requiring atmospheric correction)

**ONLY** expand: `LANDSAT/LC08/C02/T1_L2` → then check the 6 boxes listed above.
